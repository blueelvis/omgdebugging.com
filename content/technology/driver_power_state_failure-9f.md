**What the problem was**–

- Random Reboots
- Blue Screen with bug check Driver_Power_State_Failure

**System**– ASUS G750JM

**What fixed the problem**–

The owner had installed the NVIDIA Drivers from NVIDIA Site. Re-installing the drivers provided by manufacturer fixed it.
